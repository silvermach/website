What Is GreenMach?

Sustainability, engineered in.

GreenMach is Team SilverMach's dedicated sustainability programme — the set of engineering decisions, material choices and community commitments that sit alongside our race car, not apart from it. Every gram we print, every cartridge we fire and every workshop we run is logged against the same standard we hold our lap times to.

Rather than treating sustainability as a slide at the end of a sponsorship deck, we treat it as a design constraint from the first CAD sketch: minimise material, maximise reuse, and make sure the knowledge we build reaches more students than just our five-person team.

UN SDGs

Five United Nations Sustainable Development Goals sit behind every decision GreenMach makes.

Pillar One

Environmental Sustainability

Our environmental commitment starts on the CAD screen, long before any material is cut, printed or fired.

Lightweight 3D-Printed Assembly

Our final printed assembly weighs just 35–45g — every gram removed is less filament, less print time and less energy per part.

35–45grams final assembly

Recycled & Bio-Based Materials

PLA filament derived from renewable corn starch, sustainably-sourced hardwood offcuts, and CO₂ cartridge shells sent for metal recycling after every run.

100% recyclable cartridges

Digital-First Prototyping

Fusion 360 simulation and CFD let us iterate a design a dozen times in software before a single physical part is made, cutting failed-prototype waste dramatically.

Offcut & Scrap Reuse

Halo, cockpit and rib structures are built from the offcuts of the main body blank — almost no material is bought new solely for secondary parts.

Pillar Two

Social Sustainability

Engineering knowledge is only worth as much as the number of people it reaches. GreenMach exists to widen that number.

STEM Outreach at Scale

Hands-on workshops connecting our CO₂ car, CAD models and race data to physics and engineering classrooms.

150+students reached

Community Partnerships

Working with local schools and community organisations to bring engineering demonstrations to students who wouldn't otherwise see one.

3community partners

Widening Access to Engineering

Mentoring and open Q&A sessions designed to make motorsport engineering feel reachable, not exclusive, especially for first-time roboticists.

Open Knowledge Sharing

CAD files, simulation notes and this very Engineering Analysis Suite are built to be shown, explained and handed down to the next generation of students.

Pillar Three

Economic Sustainability

A sustainable programme also has to be a sustainable budget — for us and for the sponsors who back us.

In-House Manufacturing

3D printing and CNC work done on our own equipment keeps unit costs low and lets us iterate designs same-day instead of waiting on external vendors.

Measurable Sponsor Value

Every sponsorship rand is tracked against outreach reach, competition placement and material savings — reported back transparently, not just promised.

Reusable Tooling

Wheels, axles, jigs and fixtures are designed to survive multiple builds rather than being consumed once, cutting recurring spend season over season.

A Future Skills Pipeline

The students we reach today are the engineering, design and enterprise talent sponsors will want to hire tomorrow — sustainability that compounds.
