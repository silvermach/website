What Is SilverMach?

Build, breathe and bleed speed.

SilverMach is a competitive STEM Racing team competing at the STEM Racing Open Nationals 2026 — a national-level stage of the world's largest student engineering competition, endorsed by Formula 1. Beyond designing and racing a miniature F1 car, we operate across engineering, manufacturing, enterprise, branding, marketing, sponsorship acquisition, stakeholder engagement, and project management, proving that success in engineering comes from combining innovation, collaboration, communication, and leadership.

Our mission is to innovate, design, and optimise high performance engineering solutions through technical excellence, data driven development, and teamwork, demonstrating what young engineers can achieve through STEM Racing.

Precision

Every measurement and design decision is held to a standard where the smallest detail can decide the race.

Innovation

We approach every challenge with creative problem-solving, always looking for the smarter, faster way forward.

Performance

Technical excellence and continuous improvement drive everything from our CAD models to our race-day execution.

Professionalism

Inspired by modern motorsport teams, we bring strategic planning and professional execution to every discipline we touch.

Engineering Analysis Suite

Simulate the risk. Engineer with data.

Two tools straight from our design process: a Monte Carlo race-time simulator that models real-world uncertainty in every input, and a decision-lineage tracker that documents why each design iteration evolved and was selected.

Simulation Inputs
Each parameter is sampled from a normal distribution N(mean, σ) using a Box–Muller transform, run independently for every trial.
Model: a fixed launch impulse accelerates the car against quadratic aerodynamic drag over the given track length; reaction time is added on top as a fixed offset, mirroring how gate-reaction delay is recorded in real STEM Racing heats. All computation runs client-side in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
